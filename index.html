FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LabourLink before getting started.

LabourLink is completely free to register and create a profile. Workers can browse and apply for jobs at no cost. Hirers can post basic job listings for free, with premium options available for boosted visibility.
Every worker undergoes a multi-step verification: government ID check, skill assessment, and reference review. Workers can also upload certificates from recognised institutions to strengthen their profiles.
Workers set their preferred work radius. When a hirer posts a job, LabourLink automatically surfaces the closest verified workers matching the required skills. Both parties see a live distance indicator.
LabourLink has a dispute resolution team available 24/7. Both workers and hirers can raise a complaint directly through the app. Our team mediates and resolves most disputes within 48 hours.
We currently operate in 50+ cities across India including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Pune, and more. We're expanding rapidly — new cities are added every month.
